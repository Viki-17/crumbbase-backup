## TL;DR

Palantir grew out of PayPal's fraud-fighting insight that software should augment human analysts rather than replace them. After 9/11 exposed how US spy agencies couldn't share data, Peter Thiel and co. built the same 'superhuman analyst' approach into the CIA, then into war, pandemics, and banking, becoming a $350 billion intelligence powerhouse. ## Notable claims

- The US government had all the data needed to stop 9/11 but couldn't connect it across agencies. (Flight school records, suspicious money transfers, watchlist names, and flagged travel patterns all existed, but CIA, FBI, and NSA computers couldn't talk to each other; the 9/11 report called it 'a failure of imagination.') <!-- block:8d49ae7e-bf26-47aa-9bb7-1a97d4e4cd98 -->
- PayPal lost about $10 million per month to Russian card fraud. (Hackers stole card numbers, opened fake accounts, moved $500 via 'groceries' messages, and withdrew to Russian banks before Visa reversed the charges.) <!-- block:b2fb8bb4-25a9-4df1-a78c-d2471b49e214 -->
- Rule-based fraud systems were defeated within hours. (When PayPal blocked Russian IPs, hackers used American proxies; when $500 transfers were flagged, they split into two $250 transfers; when zip codes were checked, they matched the zip to the stolen card.) <!-- block:935b0316-5ab7-4d14-9b47-04e66becd452 -->
- Palantir's first CIA demo failed because engineers built for an imagined problem. (It only worked after engineers flew to Langley, sat next to actual analysts, and watched them work.) <!-- block:7fce8625-f679-4e96-a5e7-1ed1fc8a9e8f -->
- Palantir's software is said to have helped the CIA hunt down Osama bin Laden. (The video states it 'is also said to have helped the CIA hunt down Osama bin Laden himself,' alongside mapping COVID spread and predicting fraud.) <!-- block:62232e7b-2dcc-4efc-b5fd-3e45fc1d2125 -->
- One legendary client is worth more than many average ones. (The moment In-Q-Tel wrote a $2 million check, every three-letter agency in America took Palantir's calls.) <!-- block:fa9a77ae-3ba6-4aff-8726-343be348fe6e -->
