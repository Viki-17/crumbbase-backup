## TL;DR

Europe's car industry consolidated from dozens of independent makers into about six groups through a series of logical mergers and platform-sharing deals. Stellantis and Volkswagen Group now spread the same platforms, engines and gearboxes across many badges, exemplified by the Alfa Romeo Junior sharing everything underneath with Jeep and Fiat. That scale saved billions but hollowed out brand identity and turned single part flaws and diesel cheating into multi-brand catastrophes, followed by soaring warranties, job cuts and outsourcing engineering to Rivian, Leapmotor and Geely. <!-- block:e7fbb86f-0a56-4278-a3bc-8d5244a4932f -->

## Key ideas

- **Six owners for all of Europe:** In the first half of 2026 about 7.2 million cars were registered in Europe, with Volkswagen Group taking roughly a quarter and Stellantis around 15% across 11 brands. <!-- block:ce99e174-0456-44b0-81c4-c949d4146186 -->
  <!-- aliases: european auto consolidation; volkswagen group market share; stellantis brand count -->
- **Convergence by sensible steps:** Fiat bought Alfa Romeo in 1986, GM sold Opel and Vauxhall to PSA Group in 2017 for a PSA-tech swap, and PSA merged with Fiat Chrysler in January 2021 to form Stellantis promising 3.7 billion in yearly savings through convergence. PSA boss Carlos Tavares presented the Opel turnaround plan around phasing out GM platforms for PSA technology. <!-- block:d7d7fce3-82fc-42ce-be70-ae67e3ffd2b1 -->
  <!-- aliases: stellantis formation history; psa fiat chrysler merger; opel psa turnaround -->
- **Milano became Junior:** The Alfa Romeo Milano unveiled on 10 April 2024 was renamed Junior five days later after Italy objected, but it still shares its platform, petrol engine, gearbox and battery with the Jeep Avenger and Fiat 600 from the same Tychy factory. <!-- block:8004a8ff-7cf0-4dac-978d-78ed6a45d28c -->
  <!-- aliases: alfa romeo junior milano; stellantis tychy platform sharing; jeep avenger same platform -->
- **Shared platforms industry-wide:** Volkswagen Group cut from well over a dozen architectures to a handful, Stellantis is reducing about two dozen inherited platforms to four, and even entry Mercedes A-Class and Audi A3 engines are shared with Renault and Skoda Fabia relatives. <!-- block:b7db4137-3424-4e77-82ac-feba989e9c34 -->
  <!-- aliases: volkswagen platform reduction; stellantis four platforms; mercedes renault shared engine -->
- **One flaw hits five brands:** The PSA-family PureTech 1.2-litre three-cylinder ran its timing belt in oil, which could degrade, clog the oil-pump strainer, drop oil pressure and ruin engines across Peugeot, Citroen, Opel, Vauxhall and DS models. <!-- block:2657087d-f1d3-46ca-8ca7-65f48621e5d2 -->
  <!-- aliases: stellantis puretech failure; psa 1.2 belt in oil; peugeot engine fault -->
- **Scale makes cheating tempting:** Volkswagen admitted around 11 million diesel cars worldwide with test-detecting software, about 8.5 million in Europe, with total costs passing 32 billion euros across Volkswagen, Audi, Seat and Skoda models. <!-- block:7908eae8-affb-492e-a908-3066169bdc6e -->
  <!-- aliases: volkswagen dieselgate scale; vw audi seat skoda shared diesel; diesel emissions cheating -->
- **Savings boomerang into outsourcing:** Volkswagen committed billions to a partnership with American EV maker Rivian for software, Stellantis bought a large stake in Leapmotor to sell its cars in Europe, and Renault moved combustion engines into a joint venture with China's Geely. <!-- block:37746961-4276-4174-bc52-2ca43918eef4 -->
