## TL;DR

India's IT sector is undergoing a tectonic shift: staff-augmentation giants (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, Tech Mahindra) are shrinking the repetitive bottom of their engineering pyramid as AI eats high-volume, low-ambiguity work, while simultaneously paying 6-7x more for a new class of 'forward deployed engineers' who solve problems at the client's site. - **IT Workforce Forms Task Pyramid:** IT work stacks in layers: testing, boilerplate, maintenance and docs at the base, team leads above them, and project managers at the top. Each higher layer mainly coordinates the layer below. <!-- block:2e0469f0-7afc-4dca-86e2-69905e89f5f0 -->
